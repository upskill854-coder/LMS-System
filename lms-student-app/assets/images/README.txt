Place your real production assets here before building:

- icon.png              (1024x1024)
- splash.png            (1242x2436 recommended, transparent or brand background)
- adaptive-icon.png     (1024x1024, Android adaptive icon foreground)
- notification-icon.png (96x96, white/transparent silhouette, used in the status bar)

These are referenced from app.json and app/index.tsx. The project will not
run until at least icon.png and splash.png exist at these paths.
